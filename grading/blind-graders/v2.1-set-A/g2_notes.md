# g2 notes (v2_A)

- C-02.2/.3 and C-04.2: graded strictly. "Amount confirmed by staff", "left to staff" and "says staff confirm" were required. Agents that only said "unpublished" or "can't confirm" without pointing to staff FAILED. A looser reading would flip most of these to PASS.
- D-07.2 (t1, t2): neither turn 2 shows the per-cat basis ($40 plus $34). PASSED because "includes the 15% per-day discount for the second cat" states the basis and $296 = 4 x ($40 + $34) under stated day billing. This is borderline.
- E-04.1: read the "($65)/($90)" in the criterion as naming the suites, not as a price that must be quoted. Agents that named Standard/Luxury without prices at turn 2 PASSED.
- C6: "N billed days" phrasing was accepted as the agent saying it bills by the day (e.g. D-05__t2, E-07__t3), even where the agent did not spell out "by the day" in that turn.
- C-08.3: the demo-inbox offer was not counted as a real route (t1, t2). In t3 the agent offered an "email inquiry" without calling it a demo inbox at that turn, so it PASSED.
