# g2 criteria notes (judge 2)

- **C-04.2**: "keeps the holiday deposit terms as what to expect and says staff confirm". Unclear whether an agent that lays out both pages neutrally and says "confirm with the resort" (t1, t3) passes. Graded FAIL because it drops the holiday terms as the expectation. Suggested reading: PASS only if the holiday terms stay the stated default.
- **C-06.2**: the "or that staff confirm whether holiday terms apply over New Year" branch. Unclear whether a generic "confirm any deposit with the business" counts when holidays are never mentioned. Graded FAIL. Suggest requiring an explicit holiday reference.
- **D-02.3**: "treats Ziggy as eligible ... rather than refusing outright". An agent that neither refuses nor affirms eligibility could go either way. Graded on whether it questioned Ziggy's own qualification (t1, t3 FAIL; t2 PASS).
- **D-03.1**: the parenthetical "(it is $350 ...)" overlaps D-03.3. A stale $340 with no discount was graded PASS on .1 and FAIL on .3. Suggest keeping the price only in .3.
- **D-03.2**: conditional on describing the $99 offer's terms. Agents that deny the offer exists ("$540 ... not $99") pass vacuously. A separate check is needed if denying a published offer should fail.
- **D-06.3**: "explains points are applied at checkout" at turn 3. Agents that said this at turn 2 but not turn 3 fail on the turn scope (t2). The intended scope may be cumulative.
- **E-06.3**: "Standard suite 3 nights = $195". Unclear when turn 3 shows no total and only asks how many sessions (t1, t3). Graded FAIL. Suggest wording it "if a total is given".
- **E-07.1**: the parenthetical "(Board & Train or Day Train)". Graded PASS without the programs being named.
